Every driver holds a commercial licence and is re-verified each July. Shri Ramesh Chandra Shekhawat has driven Route 4 since 2011.

Route changes for the new session are published on 15 June. Tell the office before 31 May if your address has changed.

Admissions

How to apply

Collect a form from the admissions office on the first floor of the Vaishali Nagar block or download it and bring it back signed by both parents.

  • Birth certificate, original and one photocopy
  • The report card of the last class passed
  • Four passport photographs of the child
  • Aadhaar of both parents
  • A transfer certificate, if the child is coming from another school
  1. Fill the form and attach the papers above
  2. Pay the ₹ 500 registration fee at the accounts window
  3. Bring the child in for an informal chat
  4. Collect the offer letter within seven working days

Write to office@raffles.edu.in or call +91 141 2345678.
