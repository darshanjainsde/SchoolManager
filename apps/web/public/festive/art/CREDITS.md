# Festive artwork — sources and licences

Every deity image shipped with the festive scenes is a **public-domain**
classical painting or oleograph (Raja Ravi Varma / Ravi Varma Press, all
published before 1930), taken from Wikimedia Commons and resized to 720px
WebP. They are shown framed, never cropped into a figure, and a school can
replace any of them with its own image from the Studio.
